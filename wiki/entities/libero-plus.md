# LIBERO-Plus

## Identity

LIBERO-Plus is a robustness benchmark that extends LIBERO with environmental perturbations. The LAWA paper uses it for zero-shot transfer without fine-tuning on the augmented training data.

## Use in the paper

LAWA reports 74.4% micro-average success across the benchmark's perturbations. Matched Fast-WAM reaches 60.0%, and matched Joint-WAM reaches 70.4%.
