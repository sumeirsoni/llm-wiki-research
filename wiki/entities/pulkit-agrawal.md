# Pulkit Agrawal

## Overview

Pulkit Agrawal is a researcher at MIT (Improbable AI Lab) working on machine learning for embodied intelligence, sequence modeling, and representation learning. In this wiki he appears as senior author of [[dynamic-compression|Dynamic Compression in Recurrent Networks]] (with Jyothish Pari and Ryan Bahlous-Boldi), which introduces selective re-scanning of raw history as a computation-memory tradeoff for fixed-size recurrent states.

## Research Themes in This Wiki

- **Memory-efficient sequence models**: dynamic compression revises compressed recurrent state by revisiting retained raw tokens ([[iterative-refinement]])
- The work's two-level memory framing (raw storage + task-specialized working memory) connects to this wiki's recurrent-state threads: [[recirculation]], [[full-bandwidth-transformer]], and the state-tracking motivation in [[topological-trouble-with-transformers]]
