# Maria Brbić

## Overview

Associate Professor at EPFL and CIFAR Fellow in the Multiscale Human Program. Corresponding author on [[aristotelian-representation-hypothesis|Revisiting the Platonic Representation Hypothesis: An Aristotelian View]] (ICML 2026), which introduces null-calibration for representational similarity and the Aristotelian Representation Hypothesis.

## Role in This Wiki

- [[aristotelian-representation-hypothesis|Aristotelian Representation Hypothesis]] — leads work on metric-agnostic null calibration for CKA, mKNN, and related similarity measures; revisits cross-modal convergence with width/depth confounder correction.

## Research Theme

Brbić's lab (MLBio, EPFL) focuses on reliable comparison of neural representations across models, modalities, and scales — emphasizing statistical rigor when interpreting alignment scores used in transfer learning and neuro-AI benchmarking.
