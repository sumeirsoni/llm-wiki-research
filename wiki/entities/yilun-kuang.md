# Yilun Kuang

## Overview

Researcher at NYU / AMI Labs; corresponding author of [[lpwm|LpWM]]. Leads the rectified-distribution line of JEPA research, which replaces maximum-entropy dense embedding targets with sparse non-negative ones.

## Role in This Wiki

- [[lpwm|LpWM]] - first author and corresponding author (with [[yann-lecun|LeCun]]): sparse representations for world models via RDMReg, plus the one-hot linearization theory

## Research Line

Per LpWM's own citations, Kuang's related work includes:

- *Rectified LpJEPA* (arXiv:2602.01456) - joint-embedding predictive architectures with sparse maximum-entropy representations; introduces the RDMReg machinery LpWM applies to world models
- *Radial-VICReg* (arXiv:2602.14272) - radial Gaussianization for more informative representation learning
- Earlier work on efficient coding with maximum manifold capacity representations (MMCR)

These predecessors are cited by [[lpwm|LpWM]] but not yet ingested into this wiki.
